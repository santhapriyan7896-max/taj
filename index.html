    Questions

    Things people ask before booking

    Why aren't prices listed on the site?

    Because a per-head rate that's honest for 40 guests isn't honest for 600. Cost per head drops as numbers rise, and it also moves with the menu, the date and the distance to your venue. Rather than post a range and revise it upward later, we quote once — accurately — after you tell us those details.

    How far in advance should I book?

    For weddings and large functions, 3–4 weeks gives us the most flexibility. Smaller gatherings and kitty parties we can often take on a week's notice, and sometimes less — just ask.

    What's the minimum number of guests?

    We cater from around 10 guests upward. Below that we can still help with a smaller home-delivery menu.

    Do you do pure vegetarian menus?

    Yes — pure vegetarian, pure non-vegetarian, or a mix of both. Pure-veg events are cooked with separate equipment so there's no cross-contact.

    Do you cook at our venue or deliver?

    Both. On-site cooking works well where there's space and is what we recommend for live counters. Otherwise we cook in our kitchen and transport in insulated containers so it arrives hot.

    Is crockery, serving staff and cleanup included?

    In the Signature and Grand menus, yes. On the Classic they're available as add-ons — we'll list them clearly in your quote either way.

    How much advance do you need, and what's the cancellation policy?

    A booking advance confirms your date, with the balance due on the day. Cancellation terms are set out in your quote — talk to us early if plans change and we'll do what we can.

    Which areas do you serve?

    We cover the city and surrounding areas. Longer distances are possible with a travel charge — send us the venue and we'll confirm.
